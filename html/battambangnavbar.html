01 • INTRODUCTION

Where is Battambang Located?

Northwestern Cambodia

Battambang is located in the northwestern part of Cambodia. Its provincial capital is Battambang City.

The province has an area of approximately 11,702 square kilometres according to commonly cited geographic references.

Its landscape includes fertile agricultural plains, lowlands, hills, mountainous areas and floodplain areas connected to the Tonle Sap Lake.

04 • NATURAL LANDSCAPE

Mountains, Plains and Lowlands

Battambang has different types of natural landscapes that support agriculture and tourism.

Mountains and Hills

The southwestern and upland parts of Battambang include forested hills and mountainous terrain. Phnom Sampeau and Phnom Banan are notable local hills.

Fertile Plains

Large lowland areas have fertile soil suitable for rice cultivation and other agricultural activities.

Lowlands and Floodplains

Areas near the Tonle Sap basin experience seasonal changes in water levels and support agriculture and freshwater ecosystems.

05 • WATER RESOURCES

Rivers and Water Resources

Water is important for agriculture, fishing, transportation and daily life.

Sangker River

The Sangker River is one of the important waterways of Battambang. It flows through Battambang City and continues toward Tonle Sap Lake.

Tonle Sap Lake

Tonle Sap Lake is an important freshwater ecosystem in Cambodia. Its seasonal water changes influence fishing, agriculture and surrounding communities.

Water and Agriculture

Rivers, rainfall, reservoirs and irrigation systems support rice cultivation and other agricultural activities.
